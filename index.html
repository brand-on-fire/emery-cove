Slip Rental

Modern double finger berths. Extra wide fairways. Emery Cove offers 430 slips ranging from 30 to 60 feet. Cruise the center of San Francisco Bay just minutes from cast off. Enjoy stunning views of the skyline of San Francisco, Golden Gate/Bay Bridges, Alcatraz and Angel Islands. Emery Cove goes the extra nautical mile to offer all the amenities of a first-class marina.

Slip Ownership

Emery Cove is unique offering 100% fee title dock ownership (not a grounds lease) as an option to renting. Operated as a dockominium marina, save money and earn equity! Enjoy tax savings or maintenance free rental income. Secure an ideal location to berth your yacht for years to come.

Guest Docking

Cruise-in for a destination visit. Emery Cove offers short-term guest docking in the center of San Francisco Bay. Enjoy everything from fast food to eclectic world cuisine within walking distance. Free transportation to/from BART, Amtrak and around town with Emery-Go-Round. Walk the waterfront promenade along the Bay Trail and enjoy spectacular Bay Views.
